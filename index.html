What happens to your files

Most online converters upload your documents to a server. QuickDesk does the work inside your own browser.

Nothing is uploaded
PDFs, photos and recordings are processed on your device. We never see them and cannot lose them.
Small until you need more
This page is tiny. Each tool downloads its own small helper only the first time you open it, then the browser remembers it.
No limits, no watermark
Use any tool as often as you like. Results are clean files without logos.
Two things use the internet
Helper code is fetched from a public CDN on first use, and text recognition (OCR) downloads its language data once.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid plans, page limits or watermarks.

Which browsers work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Screen recording needs a computer browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). On a phone, the scanner can open the camera directly.

Can I convert Word or Excel files to PDF?

Not in the browser, because that needs the Office software itself. Open the file in Word or Excel and choose Save as PDF, then use these tools to merge, split or compress it.

Why does Compress PDF make text unselectable?

It rebuilds each page as a compressed image, which gives a much smaller file but removes selectable text. Keep your original if you need to search or copy from it.

Do invoices and text-to-PDF support Urdu or Arabic?

Not yet. Those two tools use English (Latin) letters only. Recording, OCR (English, Urdu, Arabic), images and all other PDF tools work with any language.
